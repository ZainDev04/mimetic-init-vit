Summary for the viva: we reimplemented the ViT, mimetic init and training from scratch and reproduced the main claim: 85.13 to 88.87, against the paper's 86.07 to 90.78. The gain is 3.74 against 4.71. Own experiment: the gain grows with training length on CIFAR-10 (+1.82, +2.42, +3.74), and mimetic at 50 epochs passes base at 100. Gap: position scale 2, our main suspect, did not help. The rest most likely comes from settings the paper does not report; only the authors' configuration or more seeds could settle it.
