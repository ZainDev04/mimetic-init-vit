Mimetic initialization of self-attention layers

Trockman and Kolter, ICML 2023. Reproduced on CIFAR-10 with a ViT written from scratch.

Team 404 Not Found

Syed Mohammad Huzaifa, Muhammad Maaz ur Rehman, Shaikh Muhammad Zain

We reproduced the main CIFAR-10 result of the paper Mimetic Initialization of Self-Attention Layers by Trockman and Kolter, ICML 2023. The paper has no official code, so the model, the initialization and the training loop are our own plain PyTorch code. Order of the talk: the paper, our setup, our results, our own experiment on training length, the gap to the paper, conclusions.
